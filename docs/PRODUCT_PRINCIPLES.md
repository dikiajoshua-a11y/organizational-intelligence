# Product Principles

- Evidence is not automatically truth. A report is a reported fact until corroborated or otherwise validated.
- Preserve historical facts and revisions; never overwrite organizational history.
- A numeric difference is not automatically a contradiction. Semantic comparability (metric, definition, unit, period, geography) must be established first.
- Detection is not accusation. Signals identify conditions requiring validation or management attention.
- Important signals must be evidence-backed and traceable to source material.
- Optimize for precision and management usefulness, not alert volume.
- AI may assist extraction and semantic interpretation; deterministic logic and provenance constrain organizational state.
- Real organizational evidence is the primary development test suite. Synthetic data is for controlled unit/behavior tests only.
