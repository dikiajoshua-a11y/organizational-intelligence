# Development Status

## Strong foundation

- Persistent organizational runtime
- Entities and relationships
- Facts, evidence, events, and audit history
- Deterministic signal logic
- Management actions and state transitions
- Control-center prototype
- Real-data PDM experiment using public Ugandan institutional evidence

## Major development areas

The central missing capability is autonomous reconstruction of organizational state from messy source evidence. The engine still needs stronger document ingestion, entity resolution, semantic indicator matching, cross-source reconciliation, temporal/version reasoning, dependency propagation, and continuous evidence-driven updates.

## Validation target

Use a real programme and a substantial corpus of primary-source documents. Do not hand-construct the final state. Measure extraction accuracy, entity resolution, relationship accuracy, reconciliation quality, signal precision, provenance completeness, and action usefulness.
